How to Flash:
  1. Connect a USB data cable from your computer to the boombox's USB-UART programming port.
  2. Click Install under your chosen firmware and select the USB serial port (usually CP2102 or CH340).
  3. If prompted to connect, hold the BOOT button on the ESP32-A1S while tapping RST, then release BOOT.
  4. After flashing Squeezelite-ESP32, connect to its temporary Wi-Fi hotspot, open the web UI, and pick the AudioKit (ES8388) hardware preset.
